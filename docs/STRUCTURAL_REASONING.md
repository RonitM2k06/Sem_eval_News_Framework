# Unified Structural Narrative Reasoning Engine

## Core Pipeline Architecture
$$\text{Article} \longrightarrow \text{Graph} \longrightarrow \text{Reasoning Engine} \begin{cases} \text{Counterfactual Interventions (CNS)} \\ \text{Evidence Grounding (ENS, ESS, EMS)} \\ \text{Conflict Detection (NCS)} \\ \text{Cross-Lingual Consistency (CLSC)} \\ \text{Adversarial Robustness (PRS)} \\ \text{Temporal Evolution (NPS, NVS)} \end{cases}$$

## Research Capabilities Overview
1. **Intervention Engine**: Executes local perturbations (removal, masking, role flipping).
2. **Graph Difference Engine**: Measures node/edge additions, removals, and structural change scores.
3. **Multi-Language Alignment**: Audits structural graph similarity across EN, BG, RU, HI, PT.
